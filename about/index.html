About MIPOL

Building a more connected intelligence layer for marketing.

MIPOL is an emerging intelligence company working at the intersection of Marketing Science, Data, AI, and Decision Intelligence.

Our direction

From fragmented signals to better-informed decisions.

Our long-term direction is a Marketing Decision Operating System: an adaptive intelligence layer that helps organisations connect what they do in the market with what customers experience and what the business needs to achieve.

We are building toward that vision through applied diagnostics, evidence-led engagements, and the development of modular intelligence capabilities. We are deliberate about distinguishing what the evidence shows from what still needs to be tested.
