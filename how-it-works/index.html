Evidence and action

A continuous loop between evidence and action.

Good intelligence is not just a report at the end of a process. It is a way to learn, make a decision, observe what changes, and improve the next decision.

01 / INPUT

Measure

Bring together available marketing, consumer, and business signals.

02 / CONTEXT

Understand

Interpret patterns in light of audiences, markets, and objectives.

03 / JUDGEMENT

Decide

Identify implications, trade-offs, evidence gaps, and possible actions.

04 / RESPONSE

Act

Support teams as they prioritise and implement appropriate changes.

05 / LEARNING

Measure again

Review new signals and learn what changed after action was taken.

The process is shaped around the decision at hand and the strength of available evidence. Where the data cannot support a conclusion, the gap should be made clear.
